## About Us

ANUPRESS is an independent site with honest reviews of hosting, finance products and tech, plus 48+
free calculators and converters that run entirely in your browser.

We're a small team spread across different countries. Not one person, and not a large company. We
don't put names or faces on the site on purpose. Judge us by whether a review's numbers hold up and
whether a tool gives you the right answer.

This account is the software half of that. We build things for our own work, and release the ones
that turn out to be useful to more people than us.

## What We Focus On

- **Browser-only tools.** Calculators and converters that do the maths locally. You upload nothing and you don't need an account.
- **Honest reviews.** We use a product before we write about it, and we publish the bad news even when there's an affiliate link on the page.
- **Dashboards.** Charts, KPI cards and printable reports for people who don't write code.
- **WordPress.** Plugins that take the repetitive work out of the admin screen.
- **Privacy by default.** Nothing we ship phones home. Where there's an exception, the repo says so in plain language.
- **Documentation.** Guides that cover every setting, including the ones that are tedious to explain.

## Open Source

### [Advanced Charts for Grist](https://github.com/anupress/grist-advanced-charts)

A custom widget that turns any Grist table into a website-style dashboard. 22 block types, from KPI
cards and 11 kinds of chart to searchable tables, invoices, calendars and maps. 10 one-click
industry templates. Printable A4 report layouts. You edit it in place, like a page builder.

There's no server anywhere. Every chart is computed in the viewer's browser, and your data never
leaves your Grist document.

[![The Advanced Charts demo dashboard](https://anupress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/grist-dashboard-widget-demo.webp)](https://anupress.github.io/grist-advanced-charts/?demo=1)

Try it without installing anything: [live demo](https://anupress.github.io/grist-advanced-charts/?demo=1)
· [the complete guide](https://anupress.com/advanced-charts-grist-widget-guide/)
· [template library](https://anupress.com/grist-dashboard-templates/)

### [WP Bulk Media Uploader](https://github.com/anupress/wp-bulk-media-uploader)

A free WordPress plugin for pushing 1,000+ images into the Media Library from a CSV, a ZIP archive
or a list of URLs, instead of dragging them in a few dozen at a time and waiting.
